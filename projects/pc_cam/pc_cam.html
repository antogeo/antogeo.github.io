PC Cam

Record videos and take photos from your webcam, right here in the browser.

This is a browser copy of my PC Cam desktop app, which I wrote in Python. I built it because the camera apps on my system kept crashing, and vibe coding my own recorder turned out to be faster. The desktop app needs Python to run. This version uses only JavaScript, so it works on this page with nothing to install.

Choose your camera and resolution, take photos or record as many clips as you like, then play them back and download the ones you want to keep.

Privacy: The recorded video exists only in your browser's temporary memory (RAM), on your own computer. It is never uploaded anywhere. This site is static and has no server that could receive files anyway. Everything is deleted when you leave or close this page, so download the clips you want to keep first.
